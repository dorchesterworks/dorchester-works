Our Story

From the Heights

Dorchester Works takes its name from Dorchester Heights — the hill where General Washington secretly moved 2,000 troops under cover of darkness on March 4, 1776, placing cannons that forced the British to evacuate Boston without a single shot fired. The bloodless victory. Strategy over brute force.

That's the philosophy behind every game we build. Premium mobile experiences that out-market the competition — designed with purpose, launched with precision, built to last.

Founded by a Dorchester native with an integrated marketing degree and a deep passion for American history, Dorchester Works is a mobile game studio celebrating 250 years of independence one title at a time.

Strategy First

Every game starts with a clear strategic objective — built to reach the right audience and monetize with purpose, not just hope.

Premium Quality

Accessible design, legible typography, and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across every product we ship.

Rooted in Dorchester

Born and raised in Dorchester. The name honors its most historic moment — decisive, strategic, American — and that spirit runs through everything we build.
